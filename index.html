The path

Salvage to the stars

Nine stages, from the first scrap pulled out of the ruins to a jump gate charged by a sphere of light around your sun.

  1. 01

    Salvage

    Strip the ruins, build the first drills and furnaces, ship plates up to the station.

  2. 02

    Power & automation

    Steam power, labs, steel and assemblers that build the factory for you.

  3. 03

    Logistics & oil

    Pipes, refineries, plastics and engines.

  4. 04

    Chemistry & rocketry

    Batteries, light alloys, processors and the first uranium.

  5. 05

    Nuclear & orbit

    Reactors and solar on the ground, factories in orbit overhead.

  6. 06

  7. 07

  8. 08

  9. 09

    Charge the jump gate. The next star waits.

Install

Up and running
in a minute

OS Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit
Disk ~350 MB
Saves %APPDATA%\Factory Game

  1. Download the installer

    Click Download for Windows and run FactoryGame-Setup.exe.

  2. Get past SmartScreen

    The game isn't code-signed, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info then Run anyway.

  3. Play

    It installs in a few seconds and adds a desktop shortcut. For a new version, download again and run it over the top — your saves stay.
